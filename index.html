Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any entry fee to attend? ▼

No! Entry is completely free for all verified B.Tech students. You just need to register before the 350-seat cap is filled.

Can 1st Year B.Tech students participate? ▼

Absolutely! In fact, the Non-Tech track (Trivia Quiz, UI/UX, AI Prompting, and Gaming) requires zero prior coding knowledge and is fantastic for 1st-year students looking to explore tech culture.

What do I need to bring on event day? ▼

Please bring: 1. Your College Student ID card. 2. A digital or printed copy of your Event Pass (with the QR code). 3. A laptop (recommended if participating in the Tech Track coding sprint, optional for non-tech).

Can I change my track on event day? ▼

Yes! The track selection during registration helps the organizing team estimate lab seating and quiz slots, but you are welcome to sit in on both tracks during the day.

What happens once the 350 limit is reached? ▼

Due to auditorium capacity restrictions, registration will close automatically once the 350th ticket is issued. We strongly advise registering immediately!
